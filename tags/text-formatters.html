Text Formatters

  1. Bold <b> : Used to make the text look bold
  2. Strong <strong> : Used to make the text look bold, also tells the browser that this text is important
  3. Italic <i> :This makes the text looks bit tilted/italic
  4. emphasize/em <em> :It also makes the text to look italic, but it tells the browser this text is important
  5. Highlighted <mark> : It is used to make the text appear highlighted or marked.
  6. Small<small>: This makes the text to appear small
  7. Deleted<del>:This makes the the text appear deleted/strikethrough
  8. Inserted<ins>: This is used to add a text later in the document
  9. Superscript<sup>: It is used to write text as exponential power or at the top corner of other text
  10. Superscript<sub>:It is used to write the text at the bottom corner of other text
